Fundability Ratio™ Explained

The Fundability Ratio™ is a proprietary weighted metric that evaluates financial strength, risk profile, and forward viability. It gives underwriters a single, clear number that summarizes your business’s lending potential.

  • Proprietary weighted formula: The exact computation is confidential and not publicly disclosed.
  • Forward-looking: Based on 3-year projections, not past performance alone.
  • Risk-focused: Designed to help banks quickly assess clarity and confidence.
  • Easy to interpret: Delivered with a color-coded risk band and summary.

You see the score. Banks see the confidence.
