48‑Hour Loan Proposal — Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 2026

48‑Hour Loan Proposal (“48HR,” “we,” “our,” or “us”) is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and safeguard information when you use our website, submit information through our intake form, or engage our loan‑proposal writing services.

By using our website or services, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

We collect information you voluntarily submit, including:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Business information
  • Loan request details
  • Financial documents you choose to upload
  • Any additional information provided through our intake form or email communication

1.2 Automatically Collected Information

When you visit our website, we may automatically collect:

  • IP address
  • Browser type
  • Device information
  • Pages visited
  • Time spent on the site

This information is used for analytics, security, and improving the user experience.

1.3 Cookies and Tracking

We may use cookies or similar technologies to:

  • Maintain session functionality
  • Improve site performance
  • Analyze traffic patterns

You can disable cookies in your browser settings, but some features may not function properly.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Prepare your loan proposal or underwriting package
  • Communicate with you about your project
  • Deliver documents within the 48‑hour service window
  • Improve our website and services

  • Comply with legal or regulatory obligations

We do not sell your information to third parties.

3. How We Share Your Information

3.1 With Your Permission

If you ask us to send your loan proposal to a lender, broker, or referral partner, we will do so with your explicit authorization.

3.2 Service Providers
