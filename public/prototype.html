Your Fast Track Dashboard

This Web Portal is your personal workspace for the 48‑Hour Fast Track. You can complete your Intake Form, upload supporting documents, schedule your weekend calls, track your progress, and download samples of our work — all from one place.

Step 1 — Complete Your Intake Form

Your Intake Form gives us the core details about your business, your goals, and your numbers. This is the first step of your Fast Track.

Step 2 — Upload Your Documents

Upload any supporting documents for your loan proposal: financial statements, tax returns, leases, contracts, or other relevant files.

Each upload begins with a form asking for your first and last name. This allows our system to automatically route your files into a dedicated folder under your name — creating a personalized workspace without requiring registration.

Step 3 — Schedule Your Weekend Calls

After your first payment, you will be directed to schedule your Saturday and Sunday phone calls. These calls anchor your 48‑Hour Sprint.

(Scheduling link appears automatically after payment.)

Step 4 — Track Your Progress

As you complete each step — Intake Form, document uploads, weekend calls — your Fast Track progresses. You can return to this Portal anytime during the week to upload more documents or review your materials.

Step 5 — Your Deliverables

Your lender‑aligned business plan, 3‑year pro forma, slide deck, and 60‑second pitch video will appear here on Monday at 5pm ET.

(This section unlocks automatically during delivery.)
